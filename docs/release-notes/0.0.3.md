# Lighthouse 2 0.0.3

## Your work from yesterday stays on the shelf

Projects you were working on no longer go to Quiet 15 minutes after their agents
stop. They now sit in a new **Recently completed** group for 24 hours, open
and newest first, just below Active. Close the laptop at night, open the panel in
the morning, and last night's projects are still there.

Each row in Active and Recently completed has a third line that says what the
main thread did: its AI summary if you have summaries turned on, or its title if
you don't.

## Pick how long "recent" lasts

Settings › General › Shelf › **Keep in Recently completed for** sets the window:
4, 8, 12 or 24 hours, or 2, 3 or 7 days. The shelf updates as soon as you pick a
value, even with the panel open.

## Quiet is hidden

The Quiet group no longer appears on the shelf. Search still finds every
project and thread in it. To bring the group back, turn on
Settings › General › Shelf › **Show Quiet projects**.

## Smaller changes

- Shorter text across onboarding, settings, help tips and notifications.
- The icon next to AI summaries is gone. The thread page still labels a summary
  with "AI summary", the model and its age.

## Upgrading

Your pins, paused and archived projects, and other settings carry over. Recently
completed starts at 24 hours, and Quiet starts hidden.
