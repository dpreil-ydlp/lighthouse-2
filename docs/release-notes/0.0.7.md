# Lighthouse 2 0.0.7

Catches agents that are waiting on you without saying so in a way apps can see.

- **Needs you** now also lights up when an agent finishes by asking you
  something, such as "Want me to push it?" or "I won't order it until you say
  go", even if it raised no approval or input request. Lighthouse reads the end
  of the last agent message on your Mac using fixed wording rules. Nothing is
  sent anywhere and no text is saved. The tag clears when you reply.
- The attention count is red, in the menu bar and in the panel.
- Search is a magnifier until you click it, press ⌘F or start typing; then the
  field slides open. Esc folds it back.
- The gear closes the panel and opens Settings in front of your other windows.

Settings and the store carry over from 0.0.6.
